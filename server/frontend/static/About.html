About this capstone

Helping people find a better stop along the road.

A full-stack learning project that brings dealership listings, customer reviews, and vehicle catalog information together in one place.

What it does

Useful information, clearly presented.

Browse dealership records imported from OpenStreetMap, open dealer details, read reviews, and submit a review after signing in. Vehicle makes and models are retrieved from NHTSA's vPIC API.

Listings are attributed to OpenStreetMap contributors. Vehicle data is attributed to NHTSA vPIC.
